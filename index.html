Build your StudyFlow

StudyFlow needs your real study information before it can build an adaptive plan.

1. Choose your subjects

Select the subjects you actually want StudyFlow to manage. Nothing is pre-filled with fake marks or exam dates.

Select at least one subject.

2. Enter your subject information

Your score is used as the initial mastery estimate, and required minutes is how much total study time you think this subject needs before its exam. Both update automatically as you study and record test results.

Please complete every required subject field.

3. Set your available study time

The planner will never allocate more time than the amount you give it here. Weekends are handled separately.

Enter valid study availability.

Record a test result

This updates mastery immediately, and - once you've logged two or more tests for this subject - refines how fast it predicts you forget it.
